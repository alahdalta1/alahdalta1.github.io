Tareq Al-Ahdal

Epidemiologist & Data Scientist

I’m an epidemiologist and health data scientist at Heidelberg University Hospital, working on causal inference from observational data and Bayesian spatiotemporal modeling. My doctoral work built and operated an end-to-end NLP pipeline over 462 million geotagged records across eleven European countries, modeling population negative sentiment against environmental stressors in R-INLA. I also work in Mendelian randomization — separating causal effects from socioeconomic confounding. Dissertation submitted to the Faculty of Medicine, Heidelberg University; defense expected autumn 2026.
